# Images

Place your product images here. The site expects these files:

| File | Used in |
|------|---------|
| `wicmini-hero.webp` | Product hero section (large hero image, ~900×700px) |
| `wicmini-hero-poster.webp` | Video poster fallback |
| `wicmini-core.webp` | WICMINI Core product card |
| `wicmini-fleet.webp` | WICMINI Fleet product card |

WebP format is preferred. PNG and JPG also work — just update the `src` in `components/LandingPage.tsx`.
