# Videos

Place your videos here. The site expects:

| File | Used in |
|------|---------|
| `wicmini-demo.mp4` | Product hero background (autoplay, muted, looped) |

Recommended: H.264 MP4, 1080p, compressed for web. The video plays behind the product image
with low opacity (0.18) as an ambient background effect. If the file is missing the browser
silently skips it and only the product image shows.
